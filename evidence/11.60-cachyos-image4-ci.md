## Evidence boundary

This result proves a reproducible hosted build and structurally consistent
download. It does not prove that the image boots on a PS5. It does not turn
Image 3's IW620, Bluetooth, display, session, fan, or scaling results into
Image 4 results.

The unified image retains the exact-ID-gated IW620 path and adds the in-tree
`mt7921e` path for the known PS5 MediaTek PCI ID `14c3:7961`, its packaged
firmware, and `disable_aspm=1`. MediaTek Wi-Fi has not been tested on firmware
11.60, and MediaTek Bluetooth hardware and driver behavior remain unknown.
First hardware reports must follow the
[Image 4 tester guide](../docs/cachyos1160-k73-image-4-tester-guide.md) and name
the exact image, loader, chipset, and attempt.
