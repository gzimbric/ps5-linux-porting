## Hardware boundary

The October 20 hardware run used a different, unpublished local raw image with
SHA-256
`5a904e02f6a2908be91054b7b73ebf6f891fece0edc1a88f2f9d60264d7556d4`.
That image booted with HDMI and SSH, and later live changes produced the Wi-Fi
and session-switch results. The public CI image contains those source changes,
but its raw-image hash is different. Matching `bzImage` and `initrd.img` hashes
do not transfer the local hardware result to the public image.

The first exact-artifact test must therefore record raw-image SHA-256
`25a0ef68…` before the boot. HDMI, SSH, Plasma-first startup, Wi-Fi, both
directions of the Steam/Plasma round trip, internal Bluetooth, reconnect,
coexistence, thermal policy and storage integrity remain hardware acceptance
gates for this downloadable artifact.

See the [Image 3 tester guide](../docs/cachyos1160-k73-image-3-tester-guide.md)
for the full procedure and
[the local Image 3 record](11.60-cachyos-image3-local.md) for the separate
October 20 observations.
